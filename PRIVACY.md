# StreamBox TV — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

StreamBox TV is a media player. It does not provide, sell or host any TV channels, films or series. You add your own playlist or IPTV account from a provider you are authorised to use.

## What stays on your device
- **Your IPTV logins** (server, username, password, playlist links). They are stored only on your device, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. They are sent only to the server you entered, to sign in and play.
- **Viewing data**: favourites, Continue watching positions, recent channels, viewer profiles, recordings, downloads and settings. These never leave your device unless you make a backup yourself.
- **Backups** are created only when you choose Backup, and go only where you save them.

## What is sent to other services
- **Your IPTV provider**: playback and catalogue requests, using the address and login you entered.
- **TMDB** (themoviedb.org): film and series titles, to fetch posters, ratings, cast and trailers.
- **OpenSubtitles** (opensubtitles.com): the title you are watching, only when you search for subtitles.
- **Voice search**: when you press the microphone, speech is handled by your device's speech recogniser (for example Google). StreamBox receives only the recognised text and never stores or uploads audio itself.

## What we do not do
- No account with us, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no sale or sharing of personal data.
- Crash details, if any, are kept only on your device and are shown to you. They are never uploaded automatically.

## Deleting your data
Delete a profile in the app, or clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. This removes everything StreamBox stored on the device.

## Children
Kids profiles show only content suitable for children and can be locked with a parental PIN. StreamBox does not knowingly collect information from children.
